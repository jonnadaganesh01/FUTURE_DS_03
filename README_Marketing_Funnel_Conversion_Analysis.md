# Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **marketing campaign performance and
customer conversion behavior** using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of customer responses to
marketing campaigns and helps identify patterns in conversion based on
customer demographics, contact methods, employment, education, housing,
loans, and campaign activity.

The project was completed as part of the **Future Interns -- Data
Science & Analytics Internship, Task 3**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objectives of this project are to:

-   Analyze overall customer conversion performance.
-   Calculate the marketing conversion rate.
-   Compare converted and non-converted customers.
-   Understand conversion patterns across different customer segments.
-   Analyze the effectiveness of different contact methods.
-   Identify customer characteristics associated with marketing
    outcomes.
-   Build an interactive and business-friendly Power BI dashboard.

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  KPI                                 Description
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  **Total Customers**                 Total number of customers analyzed

  **Converted Customers**             Customers who responded positively
                                      to the marketing campaign

  **Not Converted**                   Customers who did not convert

  **Conversion Rate**                 Percentage of customers who
                                      converted

  **Average Contacts**                Average number of contacts made
                                      with customers

  **Average Call Duration**           Average duration of customer calls
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

### Dashboard KPI Snapshot

-   **Total Customers:** 45K
-   **Converted Customers:** 5K
-   **Not Converted:** 40K
-   **Conversion Rate:** 11.70%
-   **Average Contacts:** 2.76
-   **Average Call Duration:** 258.16

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard contains multiple interactive visualizations:

### 1. Converted Customers by Job

Shows the number of converted customers across different job categories
and helps identify job segments with stronger conversion performance.

### 2. Education-wise Conversion

Compares marketing outcomes across education levels to understand how
education segments respond to campaigns.

### 3. Conversion by Housing Status

Analyzes customer conversion based on whether customers have a housing
loan.

### 4. Marketing Conversion Summary

Provides a clear comparison between converted and non-converted
customers.

### 5. Converted Customers by Contact Type

Analyzes conversion according to the communication channel used, such as
cellular, telephone, or unknown contact.

### 6. Conversion by Marital Status

Shows customer distribution and conversion behavior across married,
single, and divorced customers.

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

These filters allow users to explore campaign performance for specific

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

-   The overall marketing **conversion rate is 11.70%**.
-   The majority of customers did not convert, indicating an opportunity
    to improve campaign targeting and engagement.
-   **Cellular contact** represents a major communication channel among
    customers.
-   Conversion varies across different **job and education segments**.
-   Housing and loan-related customer characteristics can be used to
    further segment marketing campaigns.
-   Average customer contact frequency is approximately **2.76
    contacts**.
-   The average call duration is approximately **258 seconds**,
    providing an indication of customer engagement during calls.
